1. ABOUT BANO QABIL ECOMMERCE
Bano Qabil Ecommerce (BQE) is a premier Pakistani online retail marketplace specializing in consumer electronics, smart home accessories, computer peripherals, and daily lifestyle gadgets. Founded to deliver authentic tech products with transparent pricing across Pakistan, BQE serves retail shoppers, corporate bulk buyers, and student developers.

2. CORE SERVICES
- Nationwide Express & Standard Doorstep Delivery across 120+ cities in Pakistan.
- Cash on Delivery (COD), JazzCash, Easypaisa, Raast Instant Transfer, and Visa/Mastercard online checkout.
- 7-Day Hassle-Free Replacement Warranty on manufacturing defects.
- Dedicated B2B Corporate Procurement Desk for bulk laptop, monitor, and accessory orders.
- Live Order Tracking via SMS, WhatsApp, and the BQE Web Portal.
