COMPANY: Bano Qabil Ecommerce (BQE)

WARNING: THIS DOCUMENT IS ARCHIVED (v1.0). IT WAS SUPERSEDED BY POLICY v2.0 ON 2026-09-01. DO NOT APPLY THESE RULES TO CURRENT ORDERS UNLESS SPECIFICALLY ASKED ABOUT HISTORICAL/OLD POLICIES.

1. ARCHIVED SHIPPING CHARGES & FREE DELIVERY THRESHOLD (v1.0)
- Standard Nationwide Shipping Fee: Flat PKR 150 per order for orders below PKR 2,500.
- Free Shipping Threshold: Orders of PKR 2,500 or above qualified for FREE Standard Shipping.
- Same-Day Express Delivery: Not available under Policy v1.0.
- Cash on Delivery (COD) Limit: COD was permitted up to PKR 50,000.

2. ARCHIVED RETURN & REPLACEMENT WINDOW (v1.0)
- Standard Return Window: Customers had only 7 calendar days from the delivery date to request a return or exchange.
- Change-of-Mind Returns: Change-of-mind returns were strictly NOT accepted under Policy v1.0; only damaged or wrong items were eligible for return.

3. ARCHIVED REFUND PROCESSING TIMELINE & METHOD (v1.0)
- Refund Timeline: Refunds took 10 to 14 business days and were issued exclusively as BQE Store Credit Vouchers (cash/bank refunds were not offered in v1.0).
