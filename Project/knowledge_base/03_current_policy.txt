POLICY DOCUMENT: Return, Refund & Shipping Policy (CURRENT)
COMPANY: Bano Qabil Ecommerce (BQE)
POLICY VERSION: v2.0 | EFFECTIVE DATE: 2026-09-01 | STATUS: CURRENT

1. CURRENT SHIPPING CHARGES & FREE DELIVERY THRESHOLD
- Standard Nationwide Shipping Fee: PKR 250 per order for orders below PKR 5,000.
- Free Shipping Threshold: All orders with a cart subtotal of PKR 5,000 or above qualify for FREE Standard Nationwide Shipping.
- Same-Day Express Delivery (Karachi & Lahore only): Flat fee of PKR 450 for orders placed before 2:00 PM PKT.
- Cash on Delivery (COD) Limit: COD is available for orders up to PKR 30,000. Orders exceeding PKR 30,000 require online bank transfer, Raast, JazzCash, Easypaisa, or card prepayment.

2. CURRENT RETURN & REPLACEMENT WINDOW
- Standard Return Window: Customers may request a return or exchange within 14 calendar days from the confirmed date of delivery.
- Condition Requirement: Items must be unused, in original undamaged retail packaging, with all accessories, manuals, and unbroken security seals.
- Change-of-Mind Returns: Accepted within 14 days for unopened items, subject to a flat PKR 300 reverse-pickup logistics deduction. Defective or wrong items incur zero return fees.

3. CURRENT REFUND PROCESSING TIMELINE & METHOD
- Inspection Time: Returned parcels undergo warehouse quality verification within 2 business days of receipt.
- Refund Timeline: Approved refunds are disbursed within 5 business days directly to the customer's original payment method, IBAN bank account, JazzCash, or Easypaisa wallet.

4. EXPLICIT EXCEPTIONS TO RETURN POLICY
- Exception 1 (Hygiene & Personal Audio/Wearables): In-ear earbuds, hygiene-sealed personal care items, and opened AquaPure UV Flasks cannot be returned for change-of-mind once the hygiene seal is broken; they are eligible only for warranty replacement if defective.
- Exception 2 (Software & Activated Devices): Digital software licenses, gift vouchers, and laptops or monitors where the customer has registered/activated the manufacturer OS or warranty key are non-returnable unless a hardware fault is verified by a BQE technician.
