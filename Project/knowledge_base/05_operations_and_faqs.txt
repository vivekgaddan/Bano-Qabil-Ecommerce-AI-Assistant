1. ORDER CUT-OFF TIMES & DISPATCH PROCEDURES
- Daily Dispatch Cut-Off: Orders confirmed before 4:00 PM PKT (Monday to Saturday) are dispatched the same evening via TCS, Leopards Courier, or BQE Rider Fleet.
- Orders placed after 4:00 PM PKT or on Sundays are dispatched on the next working day.
- Estimated Delivery Times:
  * Karachi local orders: 1 to 2 working days.
  * Major cities (Lahore, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Faisalabad, Multan): 2 to 3 working days.
  * Remote areas & Gilgit-Baltistan: 4 to 6 working days.

2. ORDER CANCELLATION & ADDRESS MODIFICATION
- Customers can cancel or modify delivery addresses free of charge before the order status changes to "Dispatched" (prior to the 4:00 PM PKT cut-off).
- Once dispatched, address changes incur a courier rerouting fee of PKR 200.

3. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQs)
Q1: How do I claim a warranty replacement on a BQE product?
A: Email support@banoqabilecommerce.pk or message our WhatsApp support (+92-21-34567890) with your Order ID, invoice copy, and a 30-second video demonstrating the defect. Our team arranges a free reverse pickup within 48 hours.

Q2: Can I pick up my online order directly from the Karachi warehouse to save shipping charges?
A: Yes! Select "Self-Pickup (Karachi PECHS Hub)" at checkout. You will receive an SMS when your parcel is ready (usually within 2 hours during operating hours: Mon-Sat, 10:30 AM to 8:00 PM). Self-pickup is completely free.

Q3: Do you offer corporate discounts for bulk purchases?
A: Yes. Orders of 5 or more units of the same SKU (such as the BQE AeroBook Pro 14 Laptop or BQE ClearView 27-Inch Monitor) qualify for a 7% corporate bulk discount when ordered through b2b@banoqabilecommerce.pk.

Q4: What happens if my parcel arrives physically damaged?
A: Please record an unboxing video and report any physical transit damage within 24 hours of delivery. BQE will dispatch a brand-new replacement unit immediately at zero cost.

Q5: Does BQE offer installment plans or Buy Now Pay Later (BNPL)?
A: Currently, BQE supports 3-month and 6-month 0% markup installment plans exclusively for Meezan Bank and HBL Credit Card holders on orders above PKR 25,000.
